# The Last Signal: Support

The Last Signal is an audio horror investigation game made for VoiceOver and headphones.

## Playing with VoiceOver
- After every action, VoiceOver moves to the new story text and reads it.
- Use the rotor's Headings setting to jump between the story, your actions, objects and exits.
- Two-finger double-tap (Magic Tap) opens the pause menu. The two-finger scrub goes back out of a menu.
- The Help screen in the pause menu describes every sound the game uses.

## Settings
Text size, high contrast, volume for effects, ambience and music, haptics, and an option to have the game read the story in its own voice instead of VoiceOver.

## Problems or ideas
Email support@dungen-descent.org and tell us what happened and which chapter you were in. We read every message.
