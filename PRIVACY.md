# The Last Signal: Privacy Policy

The Last Signal does not collect, store or share any personal information.

- No accounts, no sign-in.
- No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
- No network connection is used while you play.
- Your saved games and settings are kept only on your own device. Deleting the app deletes them.

If you have a question about privacy, email support@dungen-descent.org.

Last updated: November 2026.
